How this works

  1. Run MediaPipe on a subject from the Jobs page. It finds the hand, which frames the default zoom while you label.
  2. On the Label page, place keypoints on a spread of frames and press Save & commit.
  3. Run Train from Jobs. It builds the training set, trains, and analyzes every trial.
  4. Back on Label, switch to Correct, fix the predictions that are wrong, and save.
  5. Switch to Refine, pick the corrected frames worth training on, commit, then run Refine from Jobs.
